## What's new

- **Colorblind-friendly palette.** Settings → General → Appearance has a Color profile picker. Deuteranopia moves "passed" from green to blue and leaves failures red, so the two states you need to tell apart no longer rely on red versus green. It applies to the menu bar lights, the popover, and the widget. Thanks @cwaldren-pushpress.
